004Team

Building deliberately.

Intentionally small. First hires will be senior practitioners with live transaction experience. Timing tied to engagement volume, not fundraising milestones.

  1. YEAR 1

    Senior structuring associate

    Live transaction experience across at least two of: blended finance, tax credit stacking, emerging market infrastructure, climate-aligned debt. Owns the methodology repository.

  2. YEAR 2

    Platform lead (technical)

    Founding engineer. Comfortable with finance domain modelling, retrieval over precedent transactions, and shipping products practitioners will use.

  3. YEAR 2–3

    Domestic markets lead

    Anchors affordable housing and clean energy modules. Comes from LIHTC syndication, NMTC structuring, or IRA tax credit transfer markets.

  4. YEAR 3+

    Open positions

    Additional structuring associates, head of platform partnerships, research lead. Hiring remains practitioner-first.

Working with us

Not actively hiring, but always want to hear from practitioners. Send a note to hello@example.com with two or three transactions you have worked on.
