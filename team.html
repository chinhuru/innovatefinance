004Building deliberately

Small. Deep. Practitioner first.

We are intentionally small. As we grow, every hire will be practitioner first. Senior people with live transaction experience across blended finance, tax credit stacking, emerging market infrastructure, and domestic impact markets.

We are not assembling a generalist consultancy. We are building a small, deep team that can structure what others cannot, and that knows the difference between practitioner judgment and theoretical framework.

Hiring will track engagement volume rather than fundraising milestones. Quality over headcount, every time.

Working with us

Not actively hiring, but always want to hear from practitioners. Send a note to hello@example.com with two or three transactions you have worked on.
