002The problem

Impact investing has a structuring problem, not a capital problem.

The capital exists. What does not exist, except inside a small number of large institutions, is the expertise to deploy it well.

Sophisticated structuring — the kind that designs investment strategies, builds capital stacks, and matches instruments to underlying assets — is concentrated inside the largest impact funds and DFIs. Everyone else defaults into off-the-shelf vehicles or sits on the sidelines.

The knowledge is proprietary. The structures are not.
