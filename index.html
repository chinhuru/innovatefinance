002The problem

Impact investing has a structuring problem, not a capital problem.

The capital exists. What does not exist, except inside a small number of large institutions, is the expertise to deploy it well.

A large pool of impact-motivated capital — concentrated in family offices, foundations, and mid-size advisors — sits systematically underdeployed. Not because the investors lack ambition. Because they lack access to the expertise needed to act on it.

The knowledge is proprietary. The structures are not.
