002The problem

Impact investing has a structuring gap, not a capital gap.

The capital to solve the world's most pressing challenges exists. The constraint is not supply. It is deployment.

The largest impact investors hire investment professionals from traditional finance who know how to design layered capital stacks, blended vehicles, guarantees, and outcome-linked instruments. Everyone else has three options: default to off-the-shelf vehicles, pay senior structurers deal by deal, or sit on the sidelines.

The knowledge is proprietary. The structures are not.
